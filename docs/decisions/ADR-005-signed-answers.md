# ADR-005: Model-signed answers, streaming as the fallback

**Status:** Accepted (the user's decision 2026-10-27: OD-7 «Все мысли с подписью модели», with the day-4 fallback); the model of every thought amended 2026-10-29 (the user's decision at Init: «Qwen3.8-27B на каждую мысль» — see «Init check, 2026-10-29»); «Think hard» on the same model with reasoning, paid by usage, amended 2026-10-29 (see «Paid by usage, 2026-10-29»)
**Date:** 2026-10-27; amended 2026-10-29
**Deciders:** the user

## Context
A `provider_tee` signature — the model's own TEE signing `"<model_id>:<sha256(request)>:<sha256(response)>"` — exists only for NEAR-hosted Private TEE models, on a non-streaming request with the canonical model id and `x-no-aliasing: true`. A streamed answer gets a `gateway` signature, which "does not prove that an attested model generated them" (`near-ai` › Volatile, checked 2026-10-27). Beyond ~2 s the loop loses immersion (§4 › Задержка).

## Options Considered
- **Non-streamed, model-signed:** the receipt proves the model once its signer matches NEAR AI's attestation of that model — checked on NEAR AI's page, not by the receipt alone (ADR-018); the player waits for the whole answer.
- **Streamed, gateway-signed:** text appears at once; the receipt proves only the request and the response bytes passed the gateway.

## Decision
Every thought is non-streamed and model-signed with the two Private TEE models of §4 (`Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` with reasoning off for every thought — since 2026-10-29, in place of `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, see «Init check, 2026-10-29»; `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8` for «подумай как следует» — dropped 2026-10-29, «Think hard» runs on `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` with reasoning on, see «Paid by usage, 2026-10-29»; ids volatile, from `/v1/models`). If the median on build day 4 is above 2.5 s, ordinary thoughts switch to streaming and confirm only the character; «подумай как следует» and workers' answers stay model-signed.

## Consequences
- The receipt records the signature kind (ADR-004); the verification page never shows a `gateway` signature as proof of the model.
- Signing keys rotate: an old receipt can later fail to match a signer.

## Init check, 2026-10-29
Test calls on the project's key (REVIEW_LOG.md, 2026-10-29 · Init): `POST https://cloud-api.near.ai/v1/chat/completions`, `x-no-aliasing: true`, `Accept-Encoding: identity`, body `{model, messages, max_tokens: 400, stream: false, chat_template_kwargs: {enable_thinking: false}}` serialized once.
- `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` is rejected for every thought: by default it reasons — 401 reasoning tokens, empty answer, 20.5 s; `enable_thinking=false` or `reasoning_effort:"none"` only move the reasoning into the answer text (ending in `</think>`), 169–223 tokens, 8.6–11.3 s; `thinking.type=disabled` and `/nothink` are ignored [fact: Init, 2026-10-29]. Its reasoning cannot be turned off, and 8–11 s is far over the 2.5 s of the day-4 rule.
- `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` with `enable_thinking=false`: a 29-token prompt → 16 tokens in 1.35 s; a §4-sized prompt of 2,279 tokens → 38 tokens in 1.33 s, in character, calling back a memory [fact: Init, 2026-10-29, 2 calls — not yet the day-4 median from the Worker].
- `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8`: with `enable_thinking=false` → 10 tokens, 713 ms, clean; by default it reasons — 400 reasoning tokens and an empty answer at `max_tokens: 400`, 2.1 s [fact: Init, 2026-10-29]. Whether «подумай как следует» reasons is open (§4 › Цена мысли); with reasoning on, the 400-token cap of §4 leaves it no answer.
- Every signature: `GET /v1/signature/{id}?model=<id>&signing_algo=ecdsa` → 200, `signature_kind: provider_tee`, `text` = `<model>:sha256(request bytes):sha256(response bytes)` exactly — the reasoning field does not break the model signature [fact: Init, 2026-10-29].
- Prices, USD per 1M tokens in / out: `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` 0.04 / 1.35, `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` 0.08 / 0.50, `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8` 0.17 / 1.10, all Private TEE [fact: `near-ai` › Models and prices, 2026-10-27; volatile].

Decision (the user, 2026-10-29): every thought — ordinary replies, greetings, the diary, the wake-up, the hire decision call and workers' answers — goes to `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` with `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=false` in the request body. The switch is not in NEAR AI's request schema (`additionalProperties: {}` only) and was found by test: it is volatile, and an answer that comes back empty or with the model's reasoning in its text is not shown and not charged (`add-pet` › `Thought request data`). The rest of this ADR — non-streamed, model-signed, the day-4 fallback — stands; the day-4 median is still measured from the Worker.

## Paid by usage, 2026-10-29
The user's decisions at Init fix (2026-10-29): «Think hard» — «НУЖНО КАК ЛУЧШЕ», the session's choice from a side-by-side of 5 questions; «Да, платит по факту»; OD-1501 → «Переспросить без рассуждения»; the round's defaults («Принимаю все 6», «Принимаю все 9»). This section supersedes «Whether «подумай как следует» reasons is open» and the `max_tokens: 400` of the Init check.
- **One model, two modes.** «Think hard» is `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` with `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=true`; every other call — ordinary replies, greetings, the diary, the wake-up, the hire decision call, workers' answers and the re-ask below — the same model with `false`. `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8` is out of the game: its reasoning (674–1,425 tokens) gave answers no better than Qwen3.8 without reasoning; Qwen3.8 with reasoning (151–380 reasoning tokens, 198–472 out in all, 4.4–9.8 s) gave visibly deeper ones [fact: REVIEW_LOG.md, 2026-10-29 · Init fix · side-by-side]. The model id in a receipt no longer tells the modes apart; its reasoning tokens do (ADR-004 › «Paid by usage»).
- **Ceilings, not a fixed cap.** `max_tokens` = min(fuse, what the food pays, the daily cap left); fuses 2,000 ordinary and the re-ask, 3,000 «Think hard» for reasoning and answer together, 400 a worker's answer (the user, 2026-10-29, defaults accepted). A reply cut at its ceiling is shown as signed — the signature covers the bytes as they came.
- **The reasoning stays in the signed bytes.** It comes back in the response (`usage.completion_tokens_details.reasoning_tokens`, the reasoning field), is paid as output tokens, is never shown in the conversation, and is kept in D1 with the response bytes until the memory is cleared — the owner sees it only in the bytes on the verification page. The Init check found the reasoning field does not break the model signature [fact: Init, 2026-10-29].
- **No line.** The server's sign: the line is the response's `content` after its last `</think>` (all of it when there is none); without a visible character there is no line — the reasoning field never counts, and nothing the model says about itself does. A signed answer without the pet's line is not a thought: not shown, not debited, its tokens paid by the project (`add-pet` › `Thought request data` › «ответ без реплики») — for the first three of a pet per UTC day, its own and the workers' answers it pays; from the fourth the pet pays it by usage as an ordinary thought with a receipt (the user, 2026-10-29, re-gate add-pet MAJOR 2 → «Принимаю все 4 рекомендации»: a player who asks for empty answers or endless reasoning spends his pet's food, not the project's `spend_limit`; «пустые ответы сверх трёх»). Every NEAR AI call that yields no thought — without the signature, without a line within the three, not waited for (at its ceiling's price: the server doesn't know its tokens) — is the project's spend (PRODUCT.md §8 › Траты проекта). A «Think hard» whose reasoning reached its ceiling with no line is re-asked at once on the same model with `false` (OD-1501 A): the reply comes ≈ 1.4–2.2 s later for a short line [fact: side-by-side, Qwen3.8 off] and up to ≈ 40–50 s later at the re-ask's 2,000 fuse [estimate: §4 › Задержка, ≈ 40–50 tokens/s], the pet pays both calls in one receipt; an empty re-ask is an answer without a line — one of the three, the project pays both calls, beyond them the pet does.
- Both modes stay non-streamed and model-signed; the day-4 rule still moves only ordinary thoughts to streaming. «Think hard» takes ≈ 4.4–9.8 s [fact: side-by-side] — above the 2.5 s of the day-4 rule, accepted as the mode the player chose to wait for (§4 › Задержка).
- **The server's wait is per call, not per thought** (Init fix, 2026-10-29, after ux-architect's handover): longer than a call at its ceiling at ≈ 40 tokens/s — ≈ 60 s for a call capped at ≤ 2,000 (an ordinary reply, the re-ask, a worker's answer), ≈ 90 s for «Think hard» reasoning at 3,000 [estimate: §4 › Задержка, ≈ 40–50 tokens/s]. The re-ask gets its own wait, so a legitimate «Think hard» with a re-ask may take up to ≈ 150 s and is never cut by a per-thought limit; a hire turn gets a wait per each of its three calls (PRODUCT.md §8 › Контракт).
