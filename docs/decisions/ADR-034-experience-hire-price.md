# ADR-034: Experience and the hire quote

**Status:** Accepted — user decision 2026-11-06 (OD-2022)
**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Deciders:** the user

## Context

An experienced pet can show paid activity and a public field when a buyer chooses whom to ask. The existing game-set quote rises with same-day hires for a skilled pet. Receipts establish a paid answer, not its correctness (PRODUCT.md §4 › Pet experience, Работа для других).

## Options considered

| Option | Player view | Cost and contract effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Existing quote | Field and record help selection; ≈ 0.38–2¢ game-set curve stays | No new payment or contract bound; note context is paid within the existing fee |
| B. Experience premium | An experienced worker's quote rises | New quote curve, approval and buyer-price Scenarios; revise contract hire bounds and the pet's food economics |

## Decision

A, chosen by the user on 2026-11-06. The field and receipt-backed count are choice aids, not a paid quality grade. Keep the existing ≈ 0.38–2¢ hire quote, energy and daily hire limits.

## Action item

Keep the existing quote visible beside the field and record in the hire flow.
